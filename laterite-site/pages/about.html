Our Story

Built on red earth. Trusted by builders.

A decade of hand-picking the densest, most uniform laterite stone — direct from our own quarry to your project site.

Est. 2014

From one quarry in Kannur to 500+ projects across South India.

Red Earth Co. started with a single laterite quarry in Kannur, a Tata Ace, and a simple promise: deliver stone that builders can rely on — same size, same density, every load.

Ten years later, we've grown into one of Kerala's most trusted direct-quarry laterite suppliers. We still hand-pick every batch, still own the source, and still answer the phone ourselves. The difference is scale: ten thousand lorry loads delivered, five hundred projects supplied, and a transport network that runs from Kasaragod to Bangalore.

We don't broker stone. We don't aggregate other quarries and pass them off as our own. When you buy from Red Earth Co., the stone came from our pit, sorted by our crew, and loaded on a lorry we arranged. That's the only way we know how to do it.

What We Stand For

Four promises we don't break.

These aren't slogans on a wall. They're the four reasons contractors call us back on their next project.

⛏️

Direct Quarry Supply

We own the source. No middlemen, no aggregator markups, no "sourcing partners." The stone comes from a pit we work ourselves — that's how we keep prices honest and quality consistent.

📏

Uniform Sizing, Every Load

Hand-picked and sorted at the quarry face. You won't get a 7-inch stone mixed with a 9-inch stone in the same lorry. Masons work faster, walls come out straighter, wastage drops to almost nothing.

🚛

On-Time Delivery, Guaranteed

Construction sites don't wait. Our dispatch team coordinates lorry movement the night before, and our drivers run tight routes across Kerala and up to Bangalore. Same-day or next-day dispatch is our standard.

📞

Same-Day Quotation

Send your requirement on WhatsApp — wall dimensions, stone size, delivery location. You'll have a fixed price in your hand within hours, not days. No callbacks, no runaround.
