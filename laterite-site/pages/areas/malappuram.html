Growing District

Malappuram is booming. We're keeping up with the supply.

Malappuram is one of Kerala's most populous and fastest-developing districts. New villas, townships, and commercial projects are breaking ground constantly — and the local quarry supply can't keep up.

That's where we come in. Our direct-quarry supply chain from Kannur means we can scale with your project, whether it's a single home or a 50-villa community. For Malappuram's larger developments, we offer phased delivery, locked-in rates, and credit terms for established contractors.

What we supply most to Malappuram

  • Independent villas and farmhouses (most common project type)
  • Township and gated community developments
  • Commercial buildings, especially along the Calicut–Kochi corridor
  • Religious and institutional buildings
  • Boundary walls for agricultural land conversions
