Local Knowledge

Why laterite in Kannur is different.

Kannur's laterite has a reputation for a reason. Here's what makes it the preferred choice for architects across South India.

Iron-Rich Red Tone

Kannur's laterite has one of the highest iron-oxide contents in Kerala, giving it the deep red-brown colour that architects specify by name. Other districts produce paler, more yellow-toned stone.

Denser Composition

The geological formation in North Kerala produces a tighter, denser stone with fewer voids. Less mortar absorption, better load-bearing, and longer life on exposed walls.

Better Weathering

Kannur laterite weathers more evenly than softer grades. After 20 years, an exposed Kannur wall still looks like a wall — not a crumbling façade.
