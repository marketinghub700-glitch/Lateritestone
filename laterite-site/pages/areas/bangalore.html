The same Kerala laterite that built our coast — now in Bangalore villas.

Bangalore's architects and developers have rediscovered laterite over the last five years. The aesthetic — warm red-brown, textured, naturally cooling — is exactly what modern tropical-modern design wants. And architects specifically want Kerala laterite for its colour and density, not the paler grades found locally in Karnataka.

We run dedicated long-haul loads from our Kannur quarry to Bangalore twice a week, with extra dispatches on demand. Each lorry is loaded at the quarry, sealed, and driven straight through — 350km of highway, 12–14 hours, on your site the next morning.

For multi-load projects, we offer consolidated delivery with phased scheduling. Many of our Bangalore clients (architects, developers, villa owners) have used us for 3+ projects over the years.

📐 Architect tip for Bangalore

For feature walls and exposed applications, specify the 8×8×14 grade. For long boundary walls, the 7×7×13 grade gives a more rustic, traditional Kerala-temple look that's currently very popular in Bangalore's premium gated communities.
