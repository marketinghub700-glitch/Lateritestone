Laterite Stone Supplier in Wayanad

Monsoon-grade dense laterite, supplied across Wayanad district — Kalpetta, Sulthan Bathery, Mananthavady, and Vythiri.

Built for 3,000mm of rain

Wayanad's monsoon is brutal. Our stone isn't.

Wayanad gets more rain than almost anywhere else in Kerala — over 3,000mm a year, often in 4 months straight. Cheaper laterite turns to mush in those conditions. Our Kannur-quarried stone, with its high iron-oxide content and dense cell structure, holds up where softer grades fail.

For Wayanad projects, we specifically recommend the 8×8×14 grade for above-ground walls and 15×6×4 for foundation and plinth bands. The combination handles Wayanad's moisture cycles without spalling or flaking — even on fully exposed walls without plaster.

Why Wayanad builders trust our stone

  • Iron-rich composition that resists water absorption
  • Dense cell structure — minimal capillary action
  • Excellent performance on exposed walls without plaster
  • Proven on 30+ Wayanad projects, including resorts and homestays
  • Estate walls, school buildings, and farmhouses are our core Wayanad work
