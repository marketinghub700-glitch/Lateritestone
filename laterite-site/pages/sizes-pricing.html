How pricing works

Per-piece rate + delivery. That's it.

Our pricing is intentionally simple. You pay the per-piece rate for the size you choose, plus a delivery charge based on how far your site is from our quarry. No fuel surcharge, no loading fee, no surprise "handling" charge at the end.

What affects your per-piece rate:

  • Stone size and grade (the three options above)
  • Order quantity — full lorry loads get a discount
  • Cut-to-size or custom dimensions (premium of ₹8–12/piece)

What affects your delivery charge:

  • Distance from Kannur quarry to your site
  • Within Kannur district: free on orders above ₹50,000
  • Outside Kerala (e.g. Bangalore): per-km rate, quoted upfront
