How it works

The math, briefly.

Laterite stone quantity depends on three things: wall area (length × height), the size of stone you pick, and wastage. Here's what the calculator does behind the scenes:

  1. Wall area — multiplies your length and height to get square feet.
  2. Stones per square foot — 7×7×13 needs ~11 pieces, 8×8×14 needs ~9, 15×6×4 needs ~24. This accounts for the mortar gap (typically 10mm).
  3. Wastage — we add 5% for breakages during transit and cutting on site. Kerala masons typically waste 3–7%.
  4. Lorry loads — divided by 800 pieces per lorry (the standard capacity we run). We round up so you never run short.
  5. Cost — multiplied by the per-piece rate for the size you picked. Rates reflect the current quarter.

⚠️ This is an estimate, not a quote.

The calculator assumes a standard rectangular wall with no openings, returns, or openings for doors/windows. Add 5–10% more if your wall has many openings. For a fixed quote, send us your plan on WhatsApp.
