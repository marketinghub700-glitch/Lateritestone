SEO Notes

  • Every page has unique title, meta description, canonical URL, and OpenGraph tags
  • JSON-LD structured data on every page (Organization, LocalBusiness, Product, Article, FAQ where applicable)
  • Service area pages have unique local content (not duplicated) — this is critical for local SEO
  • Blog posts target high-intent informational queries ("how to choose", "vs concrete", "cost guide")
  • All internal links use relative paths — works no matter where you host the site
  • Mobile responsive — works on phones, tablets, desktops

Integration with your existing site

  1. Drop the pages/ and assets/ folders into your current site root
  2. Add links from your homepage navigation to pages/about.html, pages/sizes-pricing.html, etc.
  3. Add an "Areas We Serve" section on the homepage that links to the 6 area pages
  4. Add a "Resources" or "Blog" link pointing to pages/blog/how-to-choose-laterite-stone.html
  5. Submit the new URLs to Google Search Console for indexing
